Audio ingestion

Start microphone streams your microphone to this server, which measures loudness every 20 ms and sends the levels back. Minimum, average, and maximum cover the latest update. 0 dBFS is the loudest signal the microphone can record; −120 dBFS is silence.

Automatic gain control, noise suppression, and echo cancellation are turned off, so the levels follow how loudly you speak.
